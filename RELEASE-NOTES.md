## Palettes v0.1.1

First public release of Palettes, a plugin for DaylightUI on World of Warcraft: Forever. It recolors DaylightUI's windows with one of 33 dark palettes and leaves your layout, class colors and combat colors alone.

### Highlights
- **33 dark palettes:** 13 Dark, 9 Class themes and 11 Realm themes
- **Accent follows class:** tint the accent to a readable shade of your class color
- **Per character or everywhere:** your pick
- **Three ways to choose:** Plugins > Palettes, the Palette dropdown on the Appearance page, or `/palettes <name>`
- **Removed:** Accent from setting.

### Install
1. You need DaylightUI installed first.
2. Download **Palettes-v0.1.1.zip** below.
3. Unzip it into `Interface/AddOns` so you have `Interface/AddOns/Palettes`.
4. In game, type `/reload`.

### Known limits
- Changes made in combat wait until combat ends.
- Editing DaylightUI's Accent or Window tint switches the palette off.
- It's v0.1.1, so expect rough edges.
