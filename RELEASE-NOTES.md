## Palettes v0.4.0

Looks is now part of Palettes. Frame styles, glow styles and ten Themes join your colours on one Plugins > Palettes page, for the DaylightUI plugin for World of Warcraft: Forever.

### Added
- **Frame and glow styles:** the seven frame styles (Lintel, Sill, Stamp, Trestle, Flagon, Signboard, Trough) and three glow styles (Wick, Draught, Latch) from Looks now live in Palettes. Pick them on the **Looks** tab, or type `/looks lintel` or `/looks wick`.
- **Themes:** ten Themes, each setting a palette, a frame style and a glow in one click: Hearthnight, Harvestide, Tidemark, Old Road, Vigil, Snug, Bellows, Drawwell, Settle and After Hours. Each row shows its palette, frame and glow by name, with the palette's swatches. Pick one on the **Themes** tab, or type `/looks hearthnight`.
- **One page, three tabs:** Plugins > Palettes now has **Themes**, **Colours** and **Looks**, each with its own reset at the top. **Restore Daylight** puts back colours, frame style and glows and clears the Theme. **Restore colours** and **Restore looks** put back only their own part.
- **PAL2 share codes:** one code carries the whole look, Theme, palette, frame and glow, like `PAL2-hearthnight-ember-lintel-wick`. Copy and Apply sit on the Colours tab. PAL1 codes still import Custom colours.

### Changed
- Change any part of a Theme and it shows as **Hearthnight (changed)**. Pick the Theme again to clear the mark.
- `/palettes off` and `/looks off` put back Daylight's colours, frame style and glows, and clear the Theme.
- The Theme, frame, glow and Restore snapshot follow **Save for this character only** and travel with Daylight profiles, just like your palette.

### Notes
- On an older DaylightUI (plugin API 1.7 or 1.8), colours still work and only the Colours tab shows.
- Using the standalone Looks add-on? Turn it off. Palettes asks you to, and its styles now live here.

### Install or update
1. You need DaylightUI installed first.
2. Download **Palettes-v0.4.0.zip** below.
3. Unzip it into `Interface/AddOns`, replacing your old `Palettes` folder.
4. If you had Looks installed, turn it off or remove its folder.
5. In game, type `/reload`.
