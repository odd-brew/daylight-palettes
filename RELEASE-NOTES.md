## Palettes v0.7.0

A major UX overhaul bringing 6 curated full-UI presets with 4 personal My Preset slots, 7 Looks frame categories with live previews (including real Action Bar shapes and borders), live Preview Deck on Colours, multi-tab staged editing, and a critical fix for client lockups when previewing presets and looks. For Palettes, the DaylightUI plugin for World of Warcraft: Forever.

### Highlights
- **6 Core Presets & My Presets:** Streamlined **Presets** to 6 distinct 8-layer setups (**Hearthnight**, **Harvestide**, **Tidemark**, **Old Road** with `Crest`, **Vigil**, and **Snug**) in a 3×2 grid, plus a live **Preset Recipe** breakdown and 4 **My Presets** slots to save and reload your own combinations.
- **7 Looks Categories:** Added single-row **Looks** sub-tabs for **Player & Target**, **Focus**, **ToT**, **Party**, **Raid**, **Cast Bars**, and **Action Bars**, synced bidirectionally with the Preview Deck, complete with dynamic Match labels and a live 8-icon Action Bars preview showing DaylightUI's real button shapes, borders, corners, shadows, and zoom.
- **Live Preview Deck on Colours:** The **Colours** tab now uses the same live DaylightUI Preview Deck as **Presets** and **Looks**.
- **Accumulating Multi-Tab Staging:** Pick a preset, swap a colour, and fine-tune individual frame looks across tabs—all your choices stay staged together until you click **Apply Live** / **Apply (Reloads UI)**, or revert cleanly with **Cancel Preview** or **Undo**. Reset buttons now stage a preview instead of immediately overwriting settings.
- **Performance & Preview Fixes:** Resolved the in-game client freeze when clicking presets or frame looks by coalescing panel refreshes during batched writes, and fixed blank Target of Target previews.

### Install or update
1. You need DaylightUI installed first.
2. Download **Palettes-v0.7.0.zip** below.
3. Unzip it into `Interface/AddOns`, replacing your old `Palettes` folder.
4. In game, type `/reload`.
