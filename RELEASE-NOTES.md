## Palettes v0.4.1

A polish pass on the Plugins > Palettes page, plus a fix for Game Menu colours. For Palettes, the DaylightUI plugin for World of Warcraft: Forever.

### Changed
- **Presets tab:** the first tab is now **Presets** (it was Themes). The tabs are Presets, Colours and Looks.
- **Preview, then Apply:** click a row on any tab to preview it, then **Apply** to keep it. The picked row fills with your accent, darkened so text stays readable, and shows a check mark. Preset rows show their palette and their frame + glow as two pills.
- **One reset per tab:** **Restore Daylight** on Presets, **Reset Colours** on Colours and **Reset Looks** on Looks, each in the tab row. The old "off" row on Colours is gone.
- Copy and spacing fixes across the page.

### Fixed
- **Game Menu colours:** the Options, Log Out and Exit Game buttons now pick up your colours. After Apply, a Custom Save, an Appearance pick or a slash command, Palettes asks **Reload to finish applying your changes?** with **Reload now** and **Later**. A change made in combat waits to ask until combat ends.

### Install or update
1. You need DaylightUI installed first.
2. Download **Palettes-v0.4.1.zip** below.
3. Unzip it into `Interface/AddOns`, replacing your old `Palettes` folder.
4. In game, type `/reload`.
