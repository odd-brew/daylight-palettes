## Palettes v0.5.1

A small wording update on DaylightUI's Appearance page. For Palettes, the DaylightUI plugin for World of Warcraft: Forever.

### Changed
- **Appearance page:** the Palettes section and its dropdown are now called **Colours**, to match the Colours tab. The description and help text now say that editing Accent or Window tint switches Palettes colours off.

### Install or update
1. You need DaylightUI installed first.
2. Download **Palettes-v0.5.1.zip** below.
3. Unzip it into `Interface/AddOns`, replacing your old `Palettes` folder.
4. In game, type `/reload`.

Found a bug? [Open an issue](https://github.com/odd-brew/daylight-palettes/issues/new/choose). Full details are in the [README](https://github.com/odd-brew/daylight-palettes#readme).
