## Palettes v0.6.0

A full DaylightUI-native menu redesign with a live Player + Target Preview Deck, instant non-destructive colour previews, and WoW Realm theme names. For Palettes, the DaylightUI plugin for World of Warcraft: Forever.

### Highlights
- **Live Player + Target Preview Deck:** **Presets** and **Looks** now display DaylightUI's real Player and Target frames side by side (vertically aligned regardless of auras or cast bars) with a synced Zoom slider. The Preview Deck hides automatically on the **Colours** tab.
- **Instant non-destructive colour previews:** Clicking any palette or preset immediately previews its colours (and frame look) live across DaylightUI without touching saved settings. Pressing Escape, switching pages, or unselecting restores your exact saved state—including any edits you made in DaylightUI's own Appearance settings.
- **DaylightUI-native rounded cards:** Replaced the old stacked rows with 2-column rounded cards on **Presets** and **Looks**, and category filter pills (**Dark**, **Class themes**, **Realm themes**, **Custom Palette**) + a 3-column swatch grid on **Colours** so every tab fits cleanly on one screen without scrolling.
- **Realm theme names:** Renamed Realm themes to **Azeroth**, **Horde**, **Alliance**, **Stormwind**, **Ironforge**, **Darnassus**, **Gnomeregan**, **Orgrimmar**, **Undercity**, **Thunder Bluff**, and **Darkspear** (existing saved profiles and presets continue working without changes).
- **Removed duplicate Share code UI:** Dropped the Share code rows from the Colours tab and Custom Palette editor in favor of DaylightUI's built-in profile sharing.

### Install or update
1. You need DaylightUI installed first.
2. Download **Palettes-v0.6.0.zip** below.
3. Unzip it into `Interface/AddOns`, replacing your old `Palettes` folder.
4. In game, type `/reload`.
