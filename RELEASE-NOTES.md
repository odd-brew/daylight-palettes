## Palettes v0.5.2

A hotfix for frame looks. For Palettes, the DaylightUI plugin for World of Warcraft: Forever.

### Fixed
- **Your DaylightUI edits stay:** since 0.5.0, a picked frame look or glow was applied again on every login and `/reload`, which overwrote changes you made in DaylightUI's own settings, such as your Player and Target frames. A look or glow now applies only when you pick it, and your later DaylightUI edits stay. A fresh install doesn't change your frames until you apply something. Restore Daylight and Reset Looks work as before.

### Install or update
1. You need DaylightUI installed first.
2. Download **Palettes-v0.5.2.zip** below.
3. Unzip it into `Interface/AddOns`, replacing your old `Palettes` folder.
4. In game, type `/reload`.
