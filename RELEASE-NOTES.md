## Palettes v0.4.3

A small fix for the Looks tab. For Palettes, the DaylightUI plugin for World of Warcraft: Forever.

### Fixed
- **Frame style preview:** clicking a frame style on the Looks tab now shows it on your player and target frames right away. Hit Apply to keep it. Leave the Looks tab, the page or the window without Apply and your saved style comes back.

### Install or update
1. You need DaylightUI installed first.
2. Download **Palettes-v0.4.3.zip** below.
3. Unzip it into `Interface/AddOns`, replacing your old `Palettes` folder.
4. In game, type `/reload`.

Found a bug? [Open an issue](https://github.com/odd-brew/daylight-palettes/issues/new/choose). Full details are in the [README](https://github.com/odd-brew/daylight-palettes#readme).
