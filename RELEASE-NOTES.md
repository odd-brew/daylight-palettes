## Palettes v0.4.2

A small fix for the Plugins > Palettes page. For Palettes, the DaylightUI plugin for World of Warcraft: Forever.

### Fixed
- **Apply button:** no longer cut off on the Presets, Colours and Looks tabs. The options list now stops above it, even after you resize the window.
- **Hover label:** hovered rows no longer show a stray "HOVER" label.

### Install or update
1. You need DaylightUI installed first.
2. Download **Palettes-v0.4.2.zip** below.
3. Unzip it into `Interface/AddOns`, replacing your old `Palettes` folder.
4. In game, type `/reload`.

Found a bug? [Open an issue](https://github.com/odd-brew/daylight-palettes/issues/new/choose). Full details are in the [README](https://github.com/odd-brew/daylight-palettes#readme).
